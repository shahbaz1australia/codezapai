Privacy Policy

30SecondSpark Publisher - Last updated October 21, 2026

30SecondSpark Publisher is a personal, single-operator tool used to publish pre-produced short videos to the TikTok account @30secondsspark on behalf of its owner. It is not a public-facing app, and it does not collect data from TikTok viewers or any third party.

What this app accesses

When the owner authorizes this app via TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token scoped only to the permissions it requests (posting video content to the authorizing account). It does not request or receive personal profile data beyond what TikTok's authorization flow provides for that purpose.

What this app does with that access

  • Uploads video files, prepared in advance by the owner, to the owner's own TikTok account via the Content Posting API.
  • Does not read, collect, or store data about other TikTok users, viewers, or accounts.
  • Does not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and video files are stored locally on the owner's own computer. No data is transmitted to or stored on any third-party server beyond TikTok's own platform, which receives only the video content being published.

Data retention and deletion

The owner may revoke this app's access at any time from their TikTok account settings, which immediately invalidates the stored access token.
